## Publications & Preprints

**Diffusion-based Artificial Genomes and Their Usefulness for Local Ancestry Inference** (2024)

*Preprint on bioRxiv*

This work explores the utility of synthetic genomes generated using diffusion models for population genetics applications, specifically evaluating their effectiveness in local ancestry inference tasks compared to real genomic data.

[Read on bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.10.28.620648v1.abstract)
