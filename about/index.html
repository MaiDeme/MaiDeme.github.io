About Me

Who am I?

I hold a Master’s degree in Bioinformatics from Université Paris-Saclay.
My main interests are genomics and evolution but I also enjoy working on various computational biology projects. I have a strong background in both Mathematics and Biology, thanks to my double Bachelor’s degree in these fields. I have just finished my Master’s final internship at the Institut de Biologie Paris-Seine where I worked in Richard Dorrell’s team on the development of two pipelines for assembly and annotation of algal genomes.

I am passionate about learning new technologies and improving my skills in programming and data analysis. I am always eager to take on new challenges and contribute to exciting projects in the field of bioinformatics.

On the personal side, I enjoy cooking and I’m a self-taught guitar player. I’ve always been fascinated by how things work — I built my own computer, love exploring technology, and even joined game jams to create games with a team. Video games have always been a big part of my journey.
