## Who am I?
I am currently working as a **Research Engineer** at the **Institut Pasteur** in Paris, France. I am part of the CNR of respiratory viruses, where I am involved into surveillance of such viruses in France. I am also working on a research project of building a method to detect reassortant influenza viruses in a surveillance context. Along this, I am also involved in various projects such as the EQA of european project AURORAE.

I hold a Master’s degree in **Bioinformatics** from Université Paris-Saclay.  
My main interests are **genomics** and **evolution** but I also enjoy working on various computational biology projects.
I have a strong background in both **Mathematics** and **Biology**, thanks to my double Bachelor’s degree in these fields.

I am passionate about learning new technologies and improving my skills in programming and data analysis. I am always eager to take on new challenges and contribute to exciting projects in the field of bioinformatics.

On the personal side, I enjoy cooking and I'm a self-taught guitar player. I’ve always been fascinated by how things work — I built my own computer, love exploring technology, and even joined game jams to create games with a team. Video games have always been a big part of my journey.
